Useful answers.

The storefront loads sample ecommerce product data from DummyJSON, including product images, titles, prices, discounts, ratings, stock, shipping and return information. Search2Get is still a portfolio frontend, not a merchant processing real orders.

Apparel receives XS–XL demo sizes, footwear receives UK sizes, and one-size accessories are labelled accordingly. Size selection is part of the frontend interaction model.

The app switches to a small built-in fallback catalog and displays a notice. Broken product images also fall back to an inline Search2Get placeholder instead of leaving empty cards.

No. Checkout validates delivery fields and demonstrates order confirmation only. No card is charged and no payment processor is connected.

Bag, wishlist, reviews, newsletter, contact and local account state use browser localStorage. They never become real server-side records.
